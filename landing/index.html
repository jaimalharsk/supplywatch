How SupplyWatch Works
01 — INGEST
Multi-source Data Pull
USGS, UN Comtrade, and sanctions feeds ingested every 6 hours. Structured, normalised, versioned.

Disruption Scoring
Per-mineral risk scores weighted by trade route exposure, price volatility, and geopolitical signals.

Threshold Alerts
Score crossings trigger webhook alerts to your systems. Configurable thresholds per mineral.

AI-generated brief every Monday: what moved, why, what to do. Delivered by email and published to Substack.
